About Us

A builder you can count on from start to finish

NR Development Group LLC is a Nederland, Texas construction and remodeling company. We work with homeowners, real estate investors, and developers who want clear communication, fair pricing, and quality work.

Every project gets a written scope, a clear contract, and itemized invoicing, so you know exactly what you're paying for and when the work will be done.

Why clients choose us

  • Written contracts and detailed scopes on every job
  • Itemized, transparent invoicing
  • Trusted, licensed trade partners
  • Direct communication with the owner
  • Clean job sites and respect for your property
